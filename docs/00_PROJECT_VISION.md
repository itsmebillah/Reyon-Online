## Vision Statement

REYON Business OS will be the unified operating platform for REYON's Beauty & Care retail business. It should connect customer-facing commerce with controlled back-office operations and trustworthy business insight, while allowing capabilities to evolve without creating disconnected sources of truth.

## Business Outcomes

The intended outcome categories are operational consistency, inventory visibility, reliable transaction records, improved customer service, controlled financial information, and decision-ready reporting. Targets are pending approval.

## Product Boundaries

The vision includes Ecommerce, Admin, POS, CRM, Inventory, Purchase, Accounts, Reports, Analytics, Automation, and AI. These names describe capability domains, not committed releases or architectural boundaries.

### Explicit Non-Decisions

- No module implementation sequence is approved here.
- No sales channel, geography, currency, tax regime, or legal entity is assumed.
- No build-versus-buy, hosting, or vendor decision is implied.

## Guiding Principles

1. Business policy is explicit, versioned, and owned.
2. Shared business concepts have governed sources of truth.
3. Operational actions are traceable to actors and evidence.
4. Security, privacy, accessibility, and financial control are design inputs.
5. Modules integrate through stable contracts rather than hidden coupling.
6. Automation and AI remain observable, reversible where practical, and human-governed.
7. Delivery proceeds in measurable increments with migration paths.

## Capability Horizon

| Horizon | Intent | Approval needed |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Confirm domains, policies, ownership, architecture, and controls. | Product and architecture sign-off |
| Core operations | Establish the minimum connected retail workflows. | Prioritized scope and acceptance criteria |
| Intelligence | Add reporting, analytics, and governed automation. | Data-quality and control readiness |
| Optimization | Apply AI to approved, measurable use cases. | Risk, privacy, and human-oversight approval |

The roadmap owns actual phasing; this table is directional only.
